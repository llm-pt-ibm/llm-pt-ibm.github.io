  • Introduction

    Welcome to the LLM-PT-IBM project blog. Here you will find reports and results from the project developed in partnership between the Federal University of Campina Grande (UFCG) and IBM, focused on evaluating language models for Portuguese. This space will provide updates on progress and analyses conducted throughout the project.
