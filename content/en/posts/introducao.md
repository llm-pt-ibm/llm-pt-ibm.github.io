Welcome to the blog of the partnership between the **Federal University of Campina Grande (UFCG)** and **IBM**!

This space brings together articles, tutorials, and research results produced by our team across different projects. Each project focuses on a distinct area of research:

- **[LLM Eval](/projects/llm-eval)** — evaluation of large language models, with a focus on benchmarks for Brazilian Portuguese.
- **[Agents](/projects/agents-ai)** — development of AI agents capable of autonomously performing multiple tasks.
- **[Judo-AI](/projects/judo-ai)** — recognition of judo technique images using computer vision and machine learning.
- **[MultiArq](/projects/multiarq)** — study and experimentation with IBM Power architecture, exploring its computational capabilities and environment configurations.

Browse the posts, filter by project, and follow the latest updates!
