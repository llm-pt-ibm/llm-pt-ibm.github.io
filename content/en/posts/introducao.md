Welcome to the blog of the partnership between the **Federal University of Campina Grande (UFCG)** and **IBM**!

This space brings together articles, tutorials, and research results produced by our team across different projects. Each project focuses on a distinct area of research:

- **[LLM Evaluation](/projects/llm-eval)** — evaluation of large language models, with a focus on benchmarks for Brazilian Portuguese.
- **[AgentOps](/projects/agents-ai)** — development of AI agents capable of autonomously performing multiple tasks.
- **[Judo-AI](/projects/judo-ai)** — use of AI models for analysis of judo matches and training sessions, applying computer vision and deep learning techniques for movement detection and action recognition.
- **[5G](/projects/5g)** — integration of AI techniques in 5G network environments, with intelligent control, optimization, and network management mechanisms.
- **[MultiArq](/projects/multiarq)** — provisioning of common tools for new architectures (ppc64le), seeking and adapting specific tools and creating technical documentation about the architecture.

Browse the posts and follow the latest updates!
